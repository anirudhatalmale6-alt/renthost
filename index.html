RentHost is a marketplace. We connect property owners and agents with professional hosts and operators. We are not the accommodation provider, we are not a guest booking site, and we do not provide or guarantee rental payments — a guaranteed rent arrangement is offered by the host and agreed directly between the two parties, subject to contract.

Owners and hosts remain responsible for their own due diligence, references, insurance, permissions and the agreement they sign. Listings and profiles are supplied by users.
